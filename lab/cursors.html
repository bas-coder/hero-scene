Cursor lab

Seven cursors built from the SVGs, lit in a procedural studio and baked to native cursors. Hover a tile to feel the real cursor. Re-bake with npm run cursors.

  1. No decorative shines or light sweeps
  2. Shadows warm grey, never violet
  3. No pixelation or banding at 1x, 2x or 3x
  4. The hotspot pixel sits on the tip
  5. One family: same camera, lights and materials
  6. A AAA product shot, not a WebGL demo
